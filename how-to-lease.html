How to lease

A short path from browsing a center to talking with leasing. We still require a background and credit check — explained in plain English below, not as a contract.

01

Browse properties

Start with the portfolio. Filter by state, city, or available size until the list matches what you need.

Open the property list

02

Pick a center

Open a center page for the address, available suites, and square footage. If a center is fully leased, you can still ask about nearby space.

03

Send an inquiry or download forms

Use the inquiry form on a center page or on Contact. To apply, download the three forms below, complete them, and send them to leasing.

Credit Check Authorization PDF Business Background PDF Personal Financial Statement PDF
04

Background and credit check

Every applicant goes through a financial background check. That usually means a look at credit history, and we may ask for more financial information. We can decline an application if the check raises concerns or if requested information is not provided. A security deposit or additional financial guarantee may be part of a lease. Submitting an application means you agree to that review — we use it only to decide whether we can offer you a space.

05

Contact leasing

Questions about a suite, a form, or timing? Start with leasing.
